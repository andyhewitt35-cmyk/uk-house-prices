About the data

  • Sold prices come from HM Land Registry's Price Paid Data for England and Wales, published monthly. Area figures use standard sales only (category A). Repossessions, buy-to-let and company sales (category B) appear in the sales list with a tag.
  • Not covered: Scotland and Northern Ireland. Their sold prices aren't published as open data (Registers of Scotland charges for it, and Northern Ireland has no open equivalent). The UK House Price Index averages below do include them.
  • Sales take weeks or months to be registered, so the newest month or two are incomplete. Area figures therefore use the 12 months to the last complete month.
  • "Typical price" is the median: half of sales were above it and half below. Small areas with few sales can swing a lot from year to year.
  • Some sales aren't in the data, such as Right to Buy discounts, gifts and transfers not at market value. A very small number of individual sales are left out of the list here, but they still count in the averages.
